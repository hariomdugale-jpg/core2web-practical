FLOWERS

FLOWERS are among the most beautiful and popular flowering plants in the world, known for their vibrant colors and elegant cup-shaped blooms. They belong to the lily family and are native to Central Asia, but they gained widespread popularity in countries like the Netherlands, where they are now a cultural symbol. Tulips come in a wide variety of colors such as red, yellow, pink, purple, and white, each often associated with different meanings like love, happiness, and purity.
